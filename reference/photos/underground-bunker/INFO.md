# Underground Bunker

## What it is
Reinforced concrete bunkers and stores, with metal grate doors, hatches and a caged shaft. Used for shelter, ammunition and supplies.

## History (draft, 2 to 4 sentences)
The fort included bunkers to protect soldiers and equipment from air and naval attack, and ammunition stores kept dry deep in the structures.

## Interesting facts (draft)
- Bunkers were designed to withstand aerial bombardment and naval fire (Wonderful Museums, unverified).
- Hatch labeled 'Merbahaya / Dangerous' in photo hatch-danger.jpg.
- Exact function of each structure needs museum signage.
