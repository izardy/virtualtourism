# Main Entrance

## What it is
The ticketed gate of the museum with the large yellow 'WAR MUSEUM' sign, flags and ticket and souvenir area, at the foot of the Bukit Batu Maung site.

## History (draft, 2 to 4 sentences)
The fort at Batu Maung was built by British Royal Engineers from 1930 (completed about 1939) to defend Penang from naval attack. It is now run as a private museum that opened to the public in 2002.

## Interesting facts (draft)
- Opened to the public in 2002 after restoration led by local businessman Johari Shafie (WARMAP).
- The site covers roughly 8 ha (about 20 acres) (MSN, Wonderful Museums).
- WARMAP describes it as the largest outdoor museum in Southeast Asia with about 100,000 visitors a year (unverified, single source).
