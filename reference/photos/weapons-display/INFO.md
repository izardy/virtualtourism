# Weapons Display

## What it is
Circular concrete gun emplacements for the fort's coastal artillery, plus other weapons displayed around the site.

## History (draft, 2 to 4 sentences)
The fort was built with gun bays for cannons and anti-aircraft positions to defend Penang from naval attack.

## Interesting facts (draft)
- The fort held cannons, anti-aircraft guns and pillboxes (WARMAP).
- The emplacements in the photos are empty circular pits with central pivot mounts.
- Gun type and calibre are unknown. Needs museum signage.
