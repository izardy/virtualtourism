# Japanese Tunnel

## What it is
Concrete tunnel passages inside the fort, including long straight corridors and a ladder shaft. The tunnels link gun positions, stores and command areas.

## History (draft, 2 to 4 sentences)
The tunnels were part of the original British fort. After the fall of Penang in December 1941 the Japanese used the site as a naval base and a detention centre. Whether the Japanese dug or extended particular tunnels is not confirmed in the sources, so the name 'Japanese Tunnel' should be checked against the museum's own labels.

## Interesting facts (draft)
- The museum spreads through old buildings, interlinking underground tunnels and fortress walls (WARMAP).
- Visitors are advised to bring a torch for darker sections (Wonderful Museums, unverified).
- The naming of this specific tunnel needs confirmation from museum signage (unverified).
