# Observation Tower

## What it is
Observation post or tower with a view over the Strait of Malacca. No photo is labeled as a tower, and sources mention observation posts rather than one named tower.

## History (draft, 2 to 4 sentences)
Observation posts with narrow slits let defenders watch the sea lanes. The hill was chosen for its wide sea view (MSN, Wonderful Museums).

## Interesting facts (draft)
- Observation posts are listed among the fort's features (MSN).
