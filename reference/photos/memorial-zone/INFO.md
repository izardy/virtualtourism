# Memorial Zone

## What it is
A memorial with a plaque 'To Our Glorious Dead' inside the museum grounds, with a small building for contemplation.

## History (draft, 2 to 4 sentences)
The memorial was sponsored by the Penang War Veterans Association and completed in 2003. It honors soldiers and civilians killed in World War I, World War II, the Malayan Emergency, the Confrontation and the Re-Insurgency period.

## Interesting facts (draft)
- Completed in 2003 (WARMAP).
- Plaque lists WWI, WWII, Malayan Railway, Malayan Emergency, Confrontation periods (visible in photo, text too small to transcribe fully).
- A small building beside it allows quiet reflection (WARMAP).

## Confirmed plaque text (from user-supplied photo ref-memorial-plaque-web.jpeg)
Black granite plaque with two engraved hibiscus flowers, standing on a stone base among plants:
- TO OUR GLORIOUS DEAD
- World War I 1914-1918
- World War II 1939-1945
- Siam-Burma Death Railway 1942-1945
- Malayan Emergency 1948-1960
- Indonesian Confrontation 1963-1966
- Re-Insurgency Period 1968-1990
- LEST WE FORGET
Note: WARMAP says the memorial was sponsored by the Penang War Veterans Association and completed in 2003. The plaque itself shows no date.
