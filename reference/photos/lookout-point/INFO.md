# Lookout Point

## What it is
A gun pit at the hill edge with a view over the sea, used as the lookout reference.

## History (draft, 2 to 4 sentences)
The hill was chosen for its unobstructed view of the sea lanes of the Strait of Malacca, giving early warning and good gun positions.

## Interesting facts (draft)
- Several lookout points offer views of the strait and the coast (Wonderful Museums, unverified).
- The photo shows a gun pit edge with the sea behind trees.
