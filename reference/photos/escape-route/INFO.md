# Escape Route

## What it is (confirmed from photos)
A vertical concrete shaft with a steel ladder, entered from a tunnel. The sign next to it reads "ESCAPE ROUTE TO TOP", with a second label "7 meters crawl, 9 meters climb" and a motivational sign "YOU CAN DO IT". A Malay safety notice ("Perhatian") is beside it. Visitors crawl about 7 m, then climb a ladder about 9 m to reach the top. The shaft is narrow (roughly shoulder width), walls are bare cast concrete with formwork marks, a pipe runs along one wall, and a rusty steel crossbar spans the top. Earlier text below was a guess.
