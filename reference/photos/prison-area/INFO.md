# Prison Area

## What it is
Small cells with barred doors and metal cages, shown to visitors as the wartime detention area.

## History (draft, 2 to 4 sentences)
After the Japanese took the fort in December 1941, parts of it were used to hold prisoners of war and captured resistance fighters. Many accounts describe executions and mistreatment. The site is locally called Bukit Hantu (Ghost Hill).

## Interesting facts (draft)
- Locally nicknamed Bukit Hantu (Ghost Hill) because of executions and reported hauntings (WARMAP). Treat the claims as local lore.
- Wall graffiti in Chinese is visible in one photo. Its translation and origin should be confirmed with the museum before it is interpreted (unverified).
- Handle with respect: this was a place of suffering.
