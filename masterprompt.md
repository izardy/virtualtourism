# PROJECT VISION

VirtualTourism is an immersive virtual reality tourism platform that allows users to explore real-world tourist attractions, heritage sites, museums, historical landmarks, and cultural destinations through highly realistic 3D environments.

The platform should deliver a Google Earth-like exploration experience but focused on high-quality destination experiences.

Users can use:

- Smartphone
- VR Cardboard Headset
- Mobile Browser
- Android App
- iOS App
- Desktop Browser

The first pilot destination is:

"Muzium Perang Penang, Malaysia"

The goal is to recreate the entire museum and surrounding environment as a detailed interactive 3D world modelled in Blender and experienced through VR.

Users should feel as if they are physically present at the location.
