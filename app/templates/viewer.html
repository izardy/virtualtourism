        How to explore

        • Guided tour: the guide walks for you. Drag (or turn your head) to look around, and use Prev, Pause and Next at the bottom.
        • Desktop: W A S D or arrow keys to walk, hold Shift to run, drag the mouse to look.
        • Phone: left joystick to walk, drag anywhere else to look.
        • Teleport: double-click or double-tap the ground.
        • Hotspots: tap a glowing marker to hear its story.
        • Cardboard VR: press Enter VR Experience, slide the phone into the headset. Tap the screen to walk forward or stop.
