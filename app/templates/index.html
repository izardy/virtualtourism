Heritage you can step into

Walk it

Move with WASD or touch controls, or teleport. A lantern lights your way through tunnels, gun bays and barracks.

Hear it

Ten glowing hotspots tell the history with photos, facts and spoken narration. Ghost stories are marked as folklore, never as fact.

Feel it in VR

Slide your phone into Google Cardboard. Split-screen 3D and head tracking work on Android, and WebXR headsets are supported.

Why virtual tourism?

Not everyone can climb a hill in the tropical heat. Schools, older visitors and people far from Penang can still walk these tunnels, learn the history, and decide whether to visit in person. Heritage sites also get a preserved record of how they look today.

This reconstruction is provisional and unofficial. Always follow the museum's own signs and guides on a real visit.
