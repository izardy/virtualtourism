Why virtual tourism?

Not everyone can travel to a heritage site or climb a hill in the tropical heat. Schools, older visitors and people far away can still walk these places, learn their history, and decide whether to visit in person. The sites also get a record of how they look today.

Reconstructions are unofficial and partly approximate. On a real visit, always follow the site's own signs and guides.
